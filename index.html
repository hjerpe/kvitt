Built on Open Payments and Zwapgrid

Both integrations run against the real sandboxes, and the application has been exercised on that real test data. Because the sandboxes lack enough history for one coherent cash story, the demo visualises the story with clearly labelled mockup data on top of the proven API access.

Verified  Open Payments: bank-side reality

  • Application authenticated with the Account Information (corporate) scope
  • Swedbank sandbox consent: SCA finalised, consent valid
  • 7 accounts, balances and 9 booked transactions retrieved
  • Feeds available cash, transaction-to-invoice matching and the "no matching payment" evidence in each recommendation

Verified  Zwapgrid: accounting-side obligations

  • Development API key verified, and the Test.1 consent reached ACCEPTED
  • 7 sales invoices from 6 customers and 3 supplier invoices retrieved
  • Reference, counterparty, dates, amount, currency and payment status
  • Feeds receivables, payables, the forecast flows and the action queue

Provenance is explicit in the product: every figure is LIVE SNAPSHOT SYNTHETIC or DERIVED, and the interface says so.

What's next: the remaining surfaces

Four categories not yet built. Each runs on the same two inputs, reconciled ERP and bank data, and feeds the same two outputs: a prioritized action and a contribution to the benchmark.

Cash Forecast CFO asks for this

A 13-week projection built from each customer's observed payment behaviour and, where there is no history, the industry's benchmark delay. Due dates lie. Behaviour does not.

Receivables CFO asks for this

Every open invoice scored against the customer's own history and its industry benchmark, so collection effort goes only to the invoices that actually move the buffer.

Payables Supporting surface

The only side of the buffer a company fully controls. Kvitt ranks which payments can slide without breaching agreed terms. Deterministic rules, never a model's guess.

Reports Supporting surface

Board- and bank-ready packs where every figure traces back to the ERP row and the bank transaction behind it, and every benchmark states how many members it was computed from.

Secure aggregation & benchmarkingMembers contribute anonymised, standardised signals. Aggregates are released only where at least five members contribute to a segment. Below that the segment is suppressed, so no single company's position can be derived. Each company receives more intelligence than it contributes.
